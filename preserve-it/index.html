PRESERVE IT

MAKE YOUR WEALTH WORK FOR YOUR LIFE.

You've worked hard to create financial security. Now it's about making sure that security actually works the way you need it to — funding the next stage of life, keeping your investments and corporate wealth aligned, and finding the efficiencies most people never get shown.

I look closely at where your money sits today — investments, corporate structures, retirement accounts — and find the specific moves that let you hold onto more of what you've built, without adding complexity to your life.

On this page, there are two moments this pillar prepares you for

Option A — If something happens to you unexpectedly

Everything may be working exactly as planned today. The business is successful. The mortgage is manageable. Investments are growing. Retirement is taking shape.

But an unexpected death can change more than a family's income. It can create immediate questions around taxes, debt, business interests, estate liquidity and how wealth will ultimately reach the people you intended it for.

Good planning helps make sure your family has time, options and financial stability—without being forced to sell assets or make major decisions at the worst possible time.

See how thoughtful planning can help preserve what happens next.

Option B — If your health changes before your plans do.

Everything may be working exactly as intended. The business is producing income. Investments are growing. Retirement is taking shape. There is a plan for what comes next.

Then illness or injury changes the timeline. Time away from work may affect more than a paycheque. It can disrupt business income, force withdrawals from investments, delay retirement plans or create pressure to use assets that were meant for another purpose.

The question becomes less: "Can we get by?" and more: "How do we protect the plan we've already built?"

Good planning creates options. It can help preserve cash flow, protect long-term assets and give you the financial flexibility to focus on recovery without unnecessarily dismantling your broader plan.
