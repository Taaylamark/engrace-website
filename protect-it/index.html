PROTECT IT

YOUR BUSINESS CREATED THE WEALTH.
PROTECT WHAT IT HAS BUILT.

For a successful business owner, the company is often much more than a source of income. It may represent decades of work, accumulated capital and a significant part of the family's wealth.

I help bring your business and personal planning together — protecting against the unexpected while also thinking ahead to retirement, corporate wealth and the eventual transition of the business.

On this page, there are two risks this pillar prepares you for

Option A — If a key person is suddenly gone

Everything may be running exactly as planned. The business is growing. The team is strong. Clients are loyal. Cash flow is steady.

But when a key owner, partner or senior person is no longer there, the impact can reach far beyond day-to-day operations. Projects may stall. Clients may become uncertain. Revenue can be disrupted. And the remaining owners may suddenly face questions about payroll, debt, ownership, succession and how to keep the business moving forward.

The real question becomes: "Does the business have the financial strength and flexibility to respond well?"

Good planning can create liquidity, protect business value and give the remaining owners time to make thoughtful decisions rather than rushed ones.

See how planning can help protect the business you've built.

Option B — If an owner or key person can no longer work

Everything may be running exactly as planned. The business is profitable. Clients are loyal. The team knows what to do. The owners are focused on growth and what comes next.

Then illness or injury changes one person's ability to contribute.

At first, the absence may seem temporary. But when weeks become months, the impact can begin to reach much further than workload. Revenue may be affected. Responsibilities may shift. Partners may need to make decisions about compensation, ownership or succession. And personal financial plans may suddenly become connected to what is happening inside the business.

The question becomes: "Does the business have enough financial flexibility to manage the transition without sacrificing what we've built?"

Good planning can help protect cash flow, preserve business value and give owners time to make thoughtful decisions instead of reacting under pressure.

See how planning can help protect the business when someone important can no longer work.
